# Code of Conduct

PulseRN follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of
private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact information on their
GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled as promptly and fairly as
possible.
